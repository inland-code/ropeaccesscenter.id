Two names often appear together in job ads: **IRATA** and **TKPK**. Both are about rope access, but they come from different systems. Knowing the difference helps you choose the right order of certification.

## The key differences

| | IRATA | Kemnaker TKPK |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Issuer | IRATA International (industry association, UK) | Indonesian Ministry of Manpower |
| Basis | Association scheme and code of practice | Regulation 9/2016 |
| Levels | Level 1, 2, 3 | TKPK 1, 2, 3 |
| Status in Indonesia | Industry recognition, often requested by international clients | National regulatory requirement |
| Proof | IRATA certificate and logbook | Competency certificate and K3 licence |

## IRATA progression rules

According to IRATA guidance, technicians can move up to Level 2 and Level 3 after logging **1,000 on-rope working hours** and at least **12 months** at the previous level. Certificates are valid for **three years** and must be revalidated. Always check the latest rules on the IRATA website before you enrol.

## TKPK levels

TKPK also has three levels, with the role growing from a worker under supervision, to a technician who can rig systems and rescue a colleague, to a supervisor who plans the work and rescue. Details are on the [TKPK Level 1](/en/tkpk-level-1-training/), [Level 2](/en/tkpk-level-2-training/), and [Level 3](/en/tkpk-level-3-training/) pages, and in [the rope access career ladder](/en/rope-access-career-indonesia/).

## Which do you need?

- **Working on projects in Indonesia** — Kemnaker TKPK is mandatory. Employers may only assign workers with a valid certificate and K3 licence, and this is what an [audit](/en/work-at-height-audit-checklist/) checks.
- **Oil and gas or multinational clients** — often ask for TKPK **and** IRATA.
- **Working abroad** — IRATA or another scheme recognised in that country is usually more relevant.

For most technicians in Indonesia, a sensible order is: start with TKPK, build up hours, then add an international certificate if the work demands it. Compare also the [Kemnaker vs BNSP](/en/kemnaker-vs-bnsp-k3-certification/) routes.
