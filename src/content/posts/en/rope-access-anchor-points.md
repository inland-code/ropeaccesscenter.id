The best harness and the most experienced technician mean nothing if the anchor fails. Indonesia's [Regulation 9/2016](/en/permenaker-9-2016-work-at-height/) even lists anchors alongside PPE and fall protection devices as a mandatory requirement.

## Types of anchor point

- **Structural anchors** — strong parts of the building such as concrete columns, steel beams, or roof frames, connected with slings or anchor straps.
- **Permanent anchors** — rings or eye bolts installed on a roof for facade maintenance. Ideally they have pull-test records and periodic inspections.
- **Temporary anchors** — devices installed for a specific job and then removed, such as beam clamps, tripods, or counterweights.

## How strong is strong enough

EN 795:2012 requires metallic anchor devices to hold a static load of **12 kN** for one user, plus 1 kN for each additional user the manufacturer permits. Devices with non-metallic load-bearing parts without proven durability are tested higher. For structural anchors chosen on site, the number is not the only measure — the condition of the concrete, corrosion, and loading direction must be assessed too.

## Rules that must not be broken

1. **Two independent systems.** The working rope and the [safety rope](/en/what-is-rope-access/) should each have their own anchor, so one failure cannot drop the worker.
2. **Correct loading direction.** Anchors are tested in a given direction. An eye bolt loaded sideways can be far weaker.
3. **Protect from sharp edges.** Slings and ropes over concrete or metal edges need protection.
4. **Keep sling angles small.** In a Y-hang, the wider the angle between the two legs, the higher the force on each anchor. Keep it below about 90°.
5. **Inspect and record.** Permanent anchors need periodic inspection, just like [other PPE](/en/rope-access-ppe-inspection/).

## The mistakes we see most often

Tying off to water pipes or gutters, using roof railings held by thin bolts, one anchor for both ropes with no backup, and unprotected slings over a parapet edge.

Selecting and building anchor systems is at the core of [TKPK Level 2](/en/tkpk-level-2-training/) and [Level 3](/en/tkpk-level-3-training/). Building managers who want their roof anchors checked can [contact us](/en/contact/).
